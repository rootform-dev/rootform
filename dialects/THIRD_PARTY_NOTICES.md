# Third-party notices

Dialect names, provider names, service names, and technology identities refer
to products owned by their respective vendors. Their inclusion describes
compatibility and does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.

This repository contains no vendor logo or icon files. Each
`presentation.json` maps Rootform rules and concepts to declarative identities;
approved visual assets remain in the proprietary Rootform renderer under their
original terms.

Provider baselines and compatibility evidence summarize public APIs and public
documentation. Upstream source URLs and versions are recorded in each
`evidence/<dialect>/` directory. Upstream code is not copied into this
repository.
